What is DataQuest?

DataQuest is an educational web application created to make statistical concepts more practical, visual, and engaging. Instead of only reading about statistical ideas, students can interact with data and observe how different representations communicate information.

The platform combines mathematical learning with web technology to provide students with opportunities to explore datasets, construct visualizations, practise concepts, and assess their understanding.
